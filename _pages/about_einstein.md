While there, I spent a whole lot of time surveying academic literature and doing research in the computer security space. During this time I also worked on implementing Office of Naval Research backed tooling into Navy systems, which is were my interest in computer security blossomed. After a few years of working on implementing academic research, I decided to go back and get my PhD so I can be the one creating novel tools!
